Grace Treasury — Marketing Screenshots
======================================
Captured at 1440×900 @2x (retina, 2880×1800 px) from a fully-seeded demo church
("Grace Fellowship Church"). All data is fictional and safe to publish.

Suggested captions / where to use each image on your website:

01-login.png
  The sign-in screen. Tagline: "Faithful stewardship, carefully recorded."
  Good for: hero / "secure, private login" section.

02-dashboard.png
  Dashboard — total across all accounts, per-account cards, Fund Balances
  (showing nested funds rolling up), and Recent Activity.
  Good for: hero screenshot / "see everything at a glance".

03-transactions.png
  Full transactions ledger with search, filters, cleared checkboxes, and
  income/expense/transfer rows.
  Good for: "Track every entry" feature section.

04-reconciliation.png
  Transactions filtered to one account with the Reconciliation bar (cleared
  balance vs. statement).
  Good for: "Bank reconciliation made simple".

05-record-transaction.png
  The Record-a-Transaction dialog (Income / Expense / Transfer).
  Good for: "Record income & expenses in seconds".

06-split-across-funds.png
  Splitting one deposit across multiple funds with a live "Fully allocated" helper.
  Good for: "Split a single gift across funds".

07-funds.png
  The Funds page — nested fund balances (parent rolls up children) plus the
  Fund Activity history (moves & adjustments).
  Good for: "Fund accounting that matches reality".

08-fund-activity.png
  Move-between-funds / adjust-a-fund dialog (no bank balance touched).
  Good for: "Reallocate money between funds".

09-treasurers-report.png
  The printable monthly Treasurer's Report: per-account income/expense detail,
  category summary, and fund balances — print or download as PDF.
  Good for: "Board-ready reports, every month".

10-settings-accounts.png
  Settings → Bank Accounts management.
  Good for: "Set up your accounts".

11-settings-funds.png
  Settings → Funds, showing opening balances and nested ("under General Fund") funds.
  Good for: "Flexible, nestable funds".

12-settings-backup.png
  Settings → Backup & Restore: manual export/restore, automatic nightly backups,
  and optional email backups.
  Good for: "Your data, safe and yours — fully self-hosted".

13-import-csv.png
  CSV import dialog for bulk-loading past months.
  Good for: "Bring your history with you".
